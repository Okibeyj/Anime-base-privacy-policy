1. Who We Are

Anime Base is an Android application provided by LIGHT READER INT'L. For privacy questions, contact contact.lightreaderpro@gmail.com.

The app stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features. - Google Advertising ID (GAID) or other advertising/device identifiers, where available and permitted.

- Ad requests, impressions and interactions.
- Technical and usage information required to operate or measure advertising services.

Yandex states that precise or approximate location is not collected by default by its Mobile Ads SDK. AppMetrica is required for the correct operation of the advertising SDK and may process technical or usage information.

- Yandex Ads Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/international_ads_privacy_policy/en/

The app may connect to anime providers such as YummyAnime, AniLiberty, AnimeGo, AnimeLib, AnimeVost, Anixart, Anichi, AniKoto, AnimePahe and KickAssAnime; video hosts such as Kodik, Aniboom, Alloha and VK; provider update services; image proxy services such as images.weserv.nl / wsrv.nl; AniSkip; and AniList if you choose to connect it.

These services may receive your IP address, content requests, episode information or other technical information necessary to provide their services. Each service has its own privacy policy and terms.

Locally stored information is used to provide your library, watch progress, offline playback, profile statistics, achievements and settings.

Third-party services may use information they receive to deliver content, operate their services, prevent abuse, display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

We do not sell your locally stored library or watch history.

5. External Content

Anime Base is a player and browser for content provided by third parties. We do not host the anime videos supplied by external providers.

When you search, stream or download content, your device communicates with the relevant provider or video host. Those services may receive your IP address and requests.

6. App Permissions

Depending on the app version and Android version, the app may request permissions for internet and network access, notifications, foreground services for background downloads, battery optimisation exemptions, local network access, installation of provider add-ons, and access to selected photos or files when you choose an avatar or banner.

You can review or change permissions in Android settings.

7. Cookies and Browser Data

Some providers use security checks that may require an embedded browser. The app may store cookies or website data locally to avoid repeating these checks.

These cookies belong to the relevant provider website, not an Anime Base account. You may clear them through available app settings, Android app storage settings or by uninstalling the app.

8. Children

Anime Base is not designed specifically for children. Third-party providers may offer content intended for mature audiences, and advertising services may apply their own age-related requirements. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app.

9. Mature Content

Some third-party providers may offer content intended for adults or subject to age restrictions. You are responsible for complying with applicable age requirements and laws. Anime Base does not host videos supplied by those providers.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

You may delete available library entries, history, downloads and profile information within the app. You can also clear app data through Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Information processed by Yandex and other third-party services is retained according to their own policies.

11. Security

Information saved by the app is intended to be stored in the app's private storage. The app uses HTTPS where supported by the relevant service.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the security practices of independent third-party services.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict processing or object to certain processing.

For information stored locally, use the deletion methods described above. For information held by third parties, contact the relevant service directly.
